What an alginate mask is

An alginate mask is made from a powder that is mixed with water just before use. The paste sets within a few minutes into a flexible layer, which I then lift off in one piece.

After a massage the skin is well circulated and most receptive to care. The mask covers it at that moment, holds in moisture and calms it. That is why I offer it as an add-on at the end of a facial massage.

How it works

  1. After the massage I apply the mask straight after your facial massage, while the skin is warm and well circulated.
  2. Application The freshly mixed paste is spread over the face in an even layer.
  3. Resting You lie back for a few minutes while the mask sets into a flexible layer.
  4. Removal I lift the mask off in one piece and finish with closing skin care.

What the alginate mask does

  • reduces puffiness and redness
  • a more even skin tone
  • deep hydration
  • calms the skin after massage
  • smoother, fresher skin
  • a quiet moment at the end of the treatment

Who it suits

The alginate mask suits dehydrated, tired or sensitive skin that tends to redden. It is also popular before an event, when you want your skin as fresh as possible.

When we do not apply it

  • allergy to any of the mask ingredients
  • open wounds or skin inflammation
  • acute skin conditions
  • recent chemical peel or laser treatment

Any facial massage - for example the facial massage, facial lymphatic massage, Gua Sha or vacuum facial massage.
