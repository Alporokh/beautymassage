What relaxation massage is

Relaxation massage — often called anti-stress massage — does not target a particular painful spot. Its job is to switch the body out of constant alert and into calm. That is why the work is slow, flowing and predictable in rhythm: the brain stops bracing for what comes next, and the nervous system finally lets go.

I use a warm aromatic oil whose scent you choose at the start. The massage travels across the whole back, legs, arms and neck and ends with a relaxing head massage. That last part is usually the strongest experience for clients — it releases tension most people did not know they were holding.

How the massage works

  1. Choosing a scent and settling You pick an aromatic oil. I dim the lights and put on quiet music.
  2. Back and neck Long flowing strokes across the whole back. This is where the breathing first slows.
  3. Legs and arms I continue to the limbs, still in the same slow rhythm with no abrupt transitions.
  4. The head massage The close belongs to the head, temples and nape. Most clients fall asleep during it.

What relaxation massage does

  • deeply calms the nervous system
  • relieves long-term stress and tension
  • markedly better sleep the very night after
  • releases stiff shoulders and neck
  • slows the pulse and breath, quiets inner restlessness
  • overall circulation and a feeling of lightness

Who it suits

Relaxation massage is ideal if you have been under stress for a long time, sleep badly, feel exhausted, or simply need an hour of complete quiet. An anti-stress massage also makes a wonderful gift for someone who never makes time for themselves.

When we do not massage

  • fever, acute inflammation or infectious illness
  • thrombosis and vein inflammation
  • fresh injury or open wounds
  • allergy to the aromatic oils used
  • cancer under active treatment

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between relaxation and classic massage?

A classic massage works deeper into the muscle and addresses specific stiff spots. Relaxation massage is slower and gentler and targets the nervous system rather than the muscle.

Can I choose the oil scent?

Yes. At the start I offer you a choice of several aromatic oils — from calming lavender to uplifting citrus.

Is a head massage included?

Yes, a relaxing head massage is part of every hour-long relaxation massage and forms its closing section.

How often should I come?

With long-term stress, once every two to four weeks is ideal. Regularity works far better here than one long massage once a year.

Can I drive afterwards?

Yes, but give yourself a few quiet minutes first. After deep relaxation your reaction time can be slower for a while.
