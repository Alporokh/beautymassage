What vacuum facial massage is

Vacuum, or cupping, facial massage uses small silicone cups that create a light suction and gently draw the skin up. Where a classic massage presses on the tissue, the cup lifts it - loosening the layers beneath the skin and getting blood and lymph moving.

On the face the cups keep moving and are never left in one place, so they do not leave marks the way cupping on the back can. The massage focuses on toning and strengthening the muscles, skin elasticity and cleaner facial lines.

How the treatment works

  1. Cleansing and oil I remove make-up and apply oil so the cups glide smoothly over the skin.
  2. Warming up by hand A short manual massage warms the skin and prepares it for the cups.
  3. Working with the cups Small cups follow smooth strokes along the jaw, cheekbones and forehead. I adjust the suction to your skin.
  4. Settling Closing manual strokes calm the skin and even out the flush.

What vacuum massage does

  • tones and strengthens the facial muscles
  • more elastic, firmer skin
  • better-defined facial contours
  • improved circulation and lymph flow
  • less puffiness
  • a fresh, brighter complexion

Who it suits

Vacuum facial massage suits you if your skin is losing elasticity, your contour is softening or you wake up with a puffy face. It is also a pleasant choice if manual facial massage feels too intense - the suction works gently, with no pressure on the bone.

When we do not massage

  • broken capillaries (couperose) or very thin skin
  • active acne, cold sores or skin inflammation
  • recent fillers or botox (wait 2–3 weeks)
  • recent chemical peel or laser treatment
  • fever and acute illness

Frequently asked questions

Will it leave marks on my face?

No. On the face the cups keep moving and the suction is light, so there are no marks like after cupping on the back. The skin may be slightly flushed for a short while afterwards.

Does vacuum massage hurt?

It does not. You feel a gentle pull and warmth. I set the suction to suit your skin and we can adjust it at any point during the massage.

Can I put make-up on straight afterwards?

I recommend giving the skin at least a few hours of rest. It is best to come without make-up, or you can remove it at the studio.

How often should I repeat it?

For a visible change in tone, a course of several sessions a week apart works well. We will plan it together based on your skin.

How is it different from Gua Sha?

Gua Sha uses a stone that glides and presses over the skin. The cup does the opposite and lifts it. Both support lymph flow and contours; they differ in feel and technique.
