What anti-cellulite massage is

Anti-cellulite massage is an intensive manual massage focused on the areas where cellulite most often settles - thighs, buttocks, hips and belly. Deep kneading and friction strokes get blood and lymph moving in tissue where circulation is slow.

After the massage the skin is flushed and firmer, and retained water drains away. Cellulite does not disappear after one session; a visible change comes with a course of massages combined with exercise and enough water.

How the treatment works

  1. A short consultation We talk about which areas bother you and how intense a massage you are comfortable with.
  2. Warm-up Opening strokes with oil warm the tissue and prepare it for deeper work.
  3. Intensive massage Kneading and friction work on the thighs, buttocks and other problem areas.
  4. Lymphatic strokes Closing strokes towards the lymph nodes help drain fluid.

What anti-cellulite massage does

  • better circulation in problem areas
  • firmer, smoother skin
  • supports fat breakdown
  • drains retained water and reduces swelling
  • lighter legs
  • gradually softer-looking cellulite over a course

Who it suits

Anti-cellulite massage is for anyone bothered by cellulite, loose skin or heavy legs. It works best as a course and combined with exercise, for example alongside vacuum body massage.

When we do not massage

  • varicose veins, phlebitis or thrombosis
  • skin conditions, wounds or inflammation in the area to be massaged
  • fever and acute illness
  • pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • a tendency to bruise or blood-thinning medication

Frequently asked questions

How many sessions do I need?

For a visible result I recommend a course of 8 to 10 massages, once or twice a week. We will plan it based on your skin.

Does it hurt?

It is intensive and you may feel pressure in sensitive spots. I always adjust the strength so it stays bearable.

Can it cause bruises?

Sensitive skin may show small bruises, especially at the start of a course. They usually fade with further sessions.

Will it remove cellulite for good?

No. The massage improves circulation and the look of the skin, but the result needs to be kept up with exercise, enough water and an occasional massage.
