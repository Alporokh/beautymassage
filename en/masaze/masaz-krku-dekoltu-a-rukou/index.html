What this massage is

The neck, décolleté and hands often show age earlier than the face - the skin there is thinner and gets less care. This massage gives them a full hour: from the back of the neck and shoulders, over the neck and décolleté, down to the forearms, palms and fingers.

The movements combine muscle release with gentle lymphatic drainage. A stiff neck and the tight chest muscles that pull us forward at the computer let go, and the skin of the neck and décolleté is well circulated and firmer.

How the treatment works

  1. Neck and shoulders I release the stiff back of the neck and shoulders, where tension from sitting holds.
  2. Neck and décolleté Gentle draining and shaping strokes over the neck and décolleté, with the lymph flow.
  3. Hands Massage of the forearms, palms and fingers to ease hands tired from computer work.
  4. Settling Slow closing strokes that calm both body and skin.

What the massage does

  • better circulation and lymph drainage
  • relaxed muscles of the neck, shoulders and chest
  • fresher, more elastic skin on the neck and décolleté
  • relaxed hands and fingers
  • relief from tension built up sitting at a computer
  • deep relaxation

Who it suits

This massage is ideal if you work at a computer and carry tension in your neck and shoulders, or want to care for the skin of the neck and décolleté, which ages along with the face. It pairs beautifully with a facial massage.

When we do not massage

  • inflammation, wounds or skin conditions in the treated area
  • thyroid conditions without a doctor's approval
  • recent neck or shoulder injuries
  • fever and acute illness
  • pregnancy in the first trimester

Frequently asked questions

Does it include the face?

No, this massage covers the neck, décolleté, shoulders and hands. If you want the face as well, choose the facial massage, which covers the neck, décolleté, face and head.

How should I prepare?

Just come in comfortable clothes, ideally without jewellery on your neck and hands. I will have everything else ready at the studio.

Will it help a stiff neck from computer work?

Yes, releasing the neck, shoulders and chest muscles is one of the main parts of the massage. For long-term back pain I also recommend the back and neck massage.

Can I combine it with a head massage?

Yes, it is a lovely combination. Mention both massages when you book and I will set aside a longer slot.
