What honey massage is

Honey massage is a traditional technique in which natural honey is applied to the skin. The palms stick to the honey and lift away against its pull - this boosts circulation in the tissues and gets the metabolism going.

At the same time the honey gently cleanses and nourishes the skin. It is most often done on the back and on areas where you want firmer, smoother skin. It is more intense than a classic massage, but leaves the skin smooth and fresh.

How the treatment works

  1. Preparation I warm the honey slightly so it is runny and pleasant on the skin.
  2. Warming up A short manual massage warms the area and prepares it for the honey.
  3. Honey massage I apply the honey and rhythmically press and lift my palms. I adjust the intensity to your sensitivity.
  4. Cleansing and settling I wipe off the remaining honey with a warm towel and calm the skin.

What honey massage does

  • cleaner, smoother skin
  • activated metabolism
  • better circulation in the tissues
  • softer, fresher skin
  • a feeling of lightness in the body
  • a good addition to anti-cellulite care

Who it suits

Honey massage suits you if you want to cleanse, soften and refresh your skin, or as part of care for problem areas. It is more intense, so I recommend it to those who are comfortable with a stronger sensation during the massage.

When we do not massage

  • allergy to honey or bee products
  • broken capillaries or varicose veins in the treated area
  • injured, irritated or inflamed skin
  • pregnancy
  • fever and acute illness

Frequently asked questions

Does honey massage hurt?

It is more intense than a classic massage - you can feel the palms pulling away. I always adjust the strength to you and we can change it at any time.

Will honey stay on my body?

No. After the massage I wipe the honey off with a warm towel. I recommend a shower in the evening.

Can the skin turn red afterwards?

Yes, brief redness is normal and is a sign of increased circulation. It usually fades within a few hours.

How often should I repeat it?

For a stronger effect, a course of several sessions a few days apart works well. We will plan it together.
