What head massage is

Head massage works on the scalp, temples, forehead and neck - the places where tension from concentration, screen work and stress builds up most during the day.

It combines circular and kneading movements on the scalp with gentle pressure on the temples and release of the neck. The scalp warms up, the muscles let go and the mind follows. Most clients leave feeling light and clear-headed.

How the treatment works

  1. Releasing the neck I start with the neck and shoulders, where tension holds the most.
  2. The scalp Circular and kneading movements over the whole scalp to warm and loosen it.
  3. Temples and forehead Gentle pressure and strokes on the temples and forehead, where tiredness and focus sit.
  4. Settling Slow closing strokes, with no rush to get up.

What head massage does

  • deep relaxation and a calmer nervous system
  • less stress and better sleep
  • a looser neck, temples and forehead
  • improved scalp circulation
  • relief from tension built up at the computer
  • a light head and a clearer mind

Who it suits

Head massage is for anyone who spends their days at a computer, carries tension in the neck and temples or cannot switch off in the evening. It also works as a short break in the middle of a demanding week or as an add-on to a facial massage.

When we do not massage

  • inflammation, wounds or skin conditions on the scalp
  • recent head or neck injuries
  • untreated high blood pressure
  • fever and acute illness
  • a severe migraine in its acute phase

Frequently asked questions

Does head massage help with headaches?

With tension and stress headaches many clients find relief, because the massage loosens the neck, temples and forehead. It does not replace a doctor though - if you have headaches often, have them checked.

Does head massage help hair grow?

The massage improves scalp circulation, which is a good environment for hair. I do not promise miracles for hair loss - for real concerns I recommend a dermatologist.

Can I go straight back to work afterwards?

Yes. Your hair may be a little tousled, but nothing stops you going straight back to your day.

Can I combine it with another massage?

Yes, it is a popular combination - for example with a facial massage or the neck, décolleté and hands massage. Just mention what you would like to combine when you book.
