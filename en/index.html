About the master

Liudmyla Yemelianova

Welcome to Massage 4 Beauty.

My name is Liudmyla, and for over ten years I have been helping my clients find harmony, relaxation and natural beauty through professional facial and body massage.

Every treatment is unique and tailored to your individual needs. Whether you are looking for deep relaxation, relief from tension, body recovery or natural skin rejuvenation, together we will find the care that is right for you.

My goal is that after your treatment you leave not only feeling light and rested, but also with renewed energy, healthier skin and a greater sense of harmony between body and soul.
